# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in the RiserBob community a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, background, experience level, identity, or technical expertise.

RiserBob is an open-source project built around learning, engineering excellence, collaboration, and the continuous improvement of software.

We expect all community members to contribute in ways that promote a positive and professional environment.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful, professional, and considerate toward others.
- Providing constructive feedback on code, architecture, and technical decisions.
- Accepting criticism and differing technical opinions gracefully.
- Welcoming newcomers and helping them understand the project.
- Focusing discussions on ideas, implementation details, and technical merit.
- Respecting the time and effort of maintainers and contributors.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Insulting, humiliating, or deliberately intimidating other community members.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or intentionally hostile discussions.
- Any other conduct that is inappropriate in a professional open-source community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all RiserBob community spaces, including:

- GitHub Issues and Pull Requests.
- Code reviews and project discussions.
- Community communication channels related to RiserBob.
- Other situations where someone is officially representing the project.

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the project maintainer through the contact information provided in the repository.

Please include relevant details, such as links to discussions, screenshots when appropriate, and a description of the incident.

Reports will be reviewed as fairly and confidentially as reasonably possible. Information will only be shared when necessary to investigate or address the issue.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Depending on the severity and circumstances of a violation, maintainers may:

1. Issue a warning and request that the behavior stop.
2. Remove inappropriate comments or contributions.
3. Temporarily restrict participation in project discussions.
4. Reject Pull Requests or close Issues that violate these standards.
5. Remove a person from project spaces for repeated or severe violations.

Maintainers will use their judgment to determine the appropriate response to each incident.

## Technical Disagreements

Technical disagreement is a normal and valuable part of software engineering.

Contributors are encouraged to challenge implementations, suggest alternatives, discuss trade-offs, and question architectural decisions.

However, criticism should focus on the code and ideas rather than attacking the people behind them.

**Disagreement is welcome. Disrespect is not.**

## Contributor Responsibilities

All contributors are expected to:

- Follow the project's contribution guidelines.
- Submit changes that are relevant to the project's goals.
- Communicate clearly and respectfully.
- Respect review decisions and project standards.
- Help maintain a safe, constructive, and collaborative community.

By participating in the RiserBob project, you agree to follow this Code of Conduct.
